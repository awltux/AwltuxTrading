Putting money in regularly regardless of what the market is doing and buying slightly more when prices fall. This is called dollar cost averaging or DCA. The concept is simple. Instead of trying to pick the perfect moment to invest a large sum, you invest a fixed amount on a regular schedule. Monthly, bi-weekly, whatever fits your situation. When prices are higher, your fixed amount buys fewer shares. When prices are lower, your fixed amount buys more shares. This means when a position in your portfolio drops significantly, say 15 or 20% from your average cost, you invest a little extra.
