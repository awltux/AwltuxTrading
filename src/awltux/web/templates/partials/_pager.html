{# The compact pager that sits at the top right of a paged table. **Only the arrows.** The pager under the table already says "page 2 of 9 (1,204 stories)", and repeating that sentence above the table would put a line the reader is about to read anyway in front of the thing they came for. What the top one answers is "is there more, and which way" *before* they scroll to the bottom -- which on the news page, at 558 pages, is the question worth asking early. The two links are passed in rather than built here, because every page derives them from different state: a FilterSet for the dashboard and the news table, a hand-built querystring for the prediction ledger and the run history, `?news_page=` for an instrument's news. A macro that built them itself would have to know all four, which is a fifth place to get paging wrong. ``None`` means "there is no such page" and renders an inert arrow rather than a link that goes nowhere. #} {% macro pager_top(prev_href, next_href, label="Table pages") %}
